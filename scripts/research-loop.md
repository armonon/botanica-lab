# Botanica Living Research Loop

Daily autonomous task:

1. Scan current health/supplement industry and botanical evidence sources:
   - FDA dietary supplement updates / warning letters
   - FTC health-products compliance/enforcement
   - NCCIH botanical safety pages
   - PubMed via NCBI E-utilities for botanicals in Botanica concepts
   - credible supplement/product trend sources when available
2. Update `sites/botanica/data/research.json` with:
   - `lastUpdated`
   - 3–7 best new `signals`
   - 3–6 strongest `remedies`
   - concise watchlist adjustments
3. Keep language internal-R&D safe:
   - no medical advice
   - no disease-treatment claims
   - no dosing instructions
   - no prescription-drug equivalence
   - clear risk flags
4. Commit and push to `armonon/botanica-lab` main.
5. Announce to Discord #mnr only when there is a meaningful new signal, a blocker, or a broken deploy.
